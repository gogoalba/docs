Cookies Cookies are small web files that a website or its service provider transfers to your device’s hard drive through your web browser, enabling the website’s or service provider’s system to recognize your browser and remember certain information. We use first‑party and third‑party Cookies for the following purposes: to ensure our Website functions properly; to improve our Website and services; to streamline your process of logging into our Website or services (e.g., remembering your user ID); to recognize you when you return to our Website; to track your interactions with the Website; to enhance your experience using the Website and services; to remember information you have provided; and to provide a secure browsing experience during your use of our Website. The length of time Cookies remain on your browsing device depends on whether they are “persistent” Cookies or “session” Cookies. Session Cookies remain on your device only until you stop browsing. Persistent Cookies remain on your browsing device until they expire or are deleted (i.e., after you finish browsing).

The Website uses the following types of Cookies:

Strictly Necessary Cookies. These Cookies are essential as they enable you to use our Website. For example, Strictly Necessary Cookies allow you to access secure areas of our Website. Without these Cookies, certain features of our Website would not be available. These Cookies do not collect your information for marketing purposes. Such Cookies are essential for the proper functioning of our Website and cannot be disabled.

Functional or Preference Cookies. We use Functional Cookies to remember your choices so that we can customize the Website to provide you with enhanced functionality and personalized content. For example, these Cookies may be used to remember your name or preferences on our Website. We do not use Functional Cookies to serve online marketing advertisements to you. While you may disable these Cookies, doing so may cause some features to malfunction during your use of our Website.

Performance or Analytical Cookies. These Cookies collect passive information about how you use our Website, including webpages you visit and links you click. We use the information collected from such Cookies to improve and optimize our Website and services. We do not use these Cookies to serve targeted advertisements to you. You may choose to disable these Cookies.
